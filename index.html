Written Response Analysis

The written responses indicate students often balance passion and financial stability. Concerns include low pay for creative fields, cost of education, and familial expectations influencing choices.

Full write-up

The hypothesis initially created indicated that students often choose careers based on prestige and average pay. This was seen throughout the responses as students often reported that their interested careers such as art, acting and teaching were low paying jobs with unstable career success. This indicates that while students are willing to pursue their passion, financial situations often dictate their choices. In addition to this, students are also worried about the expensive higher education that comes with their career choice. Although occupations such as vet and doctors pay reputable salaries, the cost of getting into post graduate school often streets students away from their dream job. This indicates that students are thinking ahead and are not willing to go into tremendous debt. Finally familial expectations still play a tremendous impact on students' choices. Familial expectations influence career consideration even when students have different personal interests. Taken together one of the most influential factors about choosing a career is the finance that comes along with it. Students are often experiencing tension between pursuing personal fulfillment or meeting financial and societal expectations. This data had also aligned with the responses from the first survey as students has expressed Finance as a top contender when choosing a career.
